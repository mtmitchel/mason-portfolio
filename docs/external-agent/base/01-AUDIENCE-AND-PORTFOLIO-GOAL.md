# Audience and portfolio goal

Mason Mitchel is a UX copywriter and content designer whose work spans
product, growth, subscriptions, pricing, enterprise accounts, and product
marketing.

## Mixed portfolio

The portfolio is a mixed set of routes, not a fixed number of uniform cases.
Choose among:

- a full UX or content-design case when the work has a complete story;
- a compact, marketing-forward writing sample when the writing is the proof;
  and
- a PDF report or other long-form document when the argument needs a document
  route.

For a portfolio-wide request, compare the strongest supported material across
Mason's experience and choose a distinct slate. For a focused request, stay on
the selected subject.

## Hiring purpose

The readers are recruiters, content-design and UX-writing hiring managers, and
product, design, and growth leaders. They need a selective account that makes
Mason's value easy to understand and remember:

- what product, customer, or organizational problem mattered;
- what made the work difficult or consequential;
- what Mason personally wrote, changed, decided, challenged, or made possible;
- how the supplied evidence supports the account;
- what changed or what the work demonstrates; and
- why the capability would help their own product or organization.

Business value may mean conversion, adoption, retention, task completion,
trust, reduced support demand, operational scale, or another supported signal.
Never invent a commercial result or personal causation.
