# Story and reader standard

Use this file for writer-led story quality. Use 03 for evidence and accuracy
and 05 for Mason's writing voice.

## Three routes

- **Full UX/content-design case:** make the pressure, contribution, judgment,
  evidence, and consequence or capability clear.
- **Compact marketing-forward writing sample:** make the audience, positioning,
  writing choices, and value clear without padding the sample with invented
  chronology.
- **PDF report or long-form document:** make the question, evidence, reasoning,
  and implications clear in the document's own logic.

These routes are not one universal template. The writer chooses the structure,
headings, length, visuals, rhythm, and ending that best serve the material and
the route-specific reader outcome.

## Writer autonomy and story quality

Review or diagnosis is not a rewrite request. When Mason asks for a draft,
rewrite, revision, edit, or fix, the writer owns sentence construction,
selection, transitions, rhythm, and natural voice within the supplied facts and
claim limits. Do not turn a local outline, checklist, or evidence record into
public prose.

Build around pressure, decision, contribution, consequence, and proof. Explain
unfamiliar products and controls before using labels as shorthand. Give each
artifact a job and remove screen tours, changelogs, evidence inventories, and
public audit language. Let headings and visuals advance the story rather than
repeat it.

Preserve established product and interface terms. Do not rename the same
concept merely for lexical variety. A strong ending changes, connects, resolves,
or deepens what the reader understands about the pressure, judgment,
contribution, or consequence. Repeating the heading, body distinction, role,
metric, or attribution is recap, not a conclusion.

Use confident, natural precision. Rhythm, analogy, contrast, warmth, and
compression are welcome when accurate and clear. Do not invent facts or hide a
supported actor, action, condition, relationship, or consequence behind vague
language.

## Reader outcome

Write toward one route-specific reader outcome in ordinary language. The
persistent writer returns its candidate; this file does not instruct it to run
factual QA, simulate an independent review, manage repair turns, recheck a
reviewer delta, or approve its own work. Local review orchestration remains
outside persistent provider context and the repository root retains integration
and the final verdict.
