## How to use the evidence

Start with the causal story, then look for the combination of artifacts that
makes it complete. Candidate rank, current route membership, an existing
central exhibit, and prior asset selection do not decide the story.

Useful combinations usually include several different jobs:

- context for the product or organizational pressure;
- evidence of Mason's role or collaboration;
- an artifact that makes the important decision visible;
- a sequence or contrast when change over time matters; and
- a measured result or other defensible ending.

One artifact may do several jobs. More artifacts are not automatically better.
