## Product and term relationships

- `DeepL Write` was the free/beta product name in the 2023 launch context.
- `DeepL Write Pro` was the full paid product or plan name in the 2024 launch
  context.
- `Write Pro` was an allowed shortened form when the Write context was already
  clear. It was not a separate product or tier.
- `DeepL Pro` referred collectively to paid Translator plans in the recovered
  launch terminology. It did not identify one plan.
- A bundle combined plans; it was not itself a plan.
- The free product state was not a paid plan.
- `Subscription` described the post-purchase relationship in contexts where
  that distinction mattered; it was not a universal synonym for `plan`.

These are historical rules. They do not establish current DeepL terminology.
