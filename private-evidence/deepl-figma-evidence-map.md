## Evidence labels

- `Correlated`: Figma and recovery evidence clearly concern the same initiative.
- `Figma-supported`: useful Figma work without a recovery match; claims remain
  bounded to the visible artifact, comments, and résumé evidence.
- `Recovery-only`: documented work without a relevant visual in these four
  files.
- `Deferred`: preserved in page 90 because purpose or duplication could not be
  proved safely.
- `Private-only`: valuable material that is unsafe to export without redaction.

## Required bidirectional crosswalk contract

This map is the private bridge between the DeepL achievement recovery and the
live Figma evidence. It is not just a list of loose thematic associations.

- Achievement to Figma: every relevant recovery record must be checked for an
  exact supporting, contextual, contradicting, or absent Figma page/frame/node.
- Figma to achievement: every relevant Figma cluster must be checked against the
  recovery ledger, curated reports, retained user-authored source messages, and
  Mason-authored role corrections.
- Exact matches must retain the recovery ID and evidence level, recovery source
  reference, immutable Figma source URL, current destination URL, page and frame
  names, node ID/node URL, relationship type, supported claim dimension,
  confidentiality limit, claim ceiling, and unresolved evidence.
- Relationship types are `direct`, `correlated`, `contextual`, `conflicting`,
  `recovery-only`, `Figma-only`, and `unresolved`. Do not force uncertain matches.
- Artifact/state, Mason's contribution, lifecycle, and outcome are distinct claim
  dimensions. Figma and recovery evidence may reinforce different dimensions,
  but neither may be used as circular proof of the other.
- A matching Figma frame does not recover a missing analytics or experiment
  attachment, prove shipment or causation, or establish sole ownership. A
  recovery mention does not prove the exact visual implementation or turn a
  flattened artifact into active editable design.
- When files are split, pages renamed, frames moved, or canonical nodes change,
  update the current destination here while preserving immutable-source
  provenance. Record unmatched evidence on both sides rather than deleting it.
