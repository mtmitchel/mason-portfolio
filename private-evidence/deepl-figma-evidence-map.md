## Authority limit

`Canonical` means only the current organizing destination inside the recovered
Figma workspace. It does not mean shipped, final, accurate in every detail,
untouchable, or the ultimate source of truth.

- Every frame, page, mockup, component, and apparent near-production state has
  a margin of error.
- Figma may preserve exploration, stale states, hybrids, aliases, incomplete
  handoff material, or work that differs from production.
- A crosswalk records the strongest current relationship, not a permanent
  verdict. Stronger direct project, approval, release, public-product, or Mason
  correction evidence may revise it.
- No future story, asset selection, caption, or claim should treat a Figma
  state as beyond challenge.

## Evidence contract

- Check both directions: achievement to Figma and Figma to achievement.
- Keep artifact, contribution, lifecycle, and outcome independent.
- Figma metadata alone may not identify an exact contributor. That narrow
  limitation does not override Mason's account of his collaborative work.
  When Mason connects himself to a recovered project or artifact family, use
  that account plus the surrounding project context to establish a reasonable
  contribution scope. Do not require a second source before the work can be
  considered or selected.
- Use `direct`, `correlated`, `contextual`, `conflicting`, `recovery-only`,
  `Figma-only`, or `unresolved`; do not force an exact match.
- A visible frame alone does not prove verbatim authorship, shipment,
  selection, adoption, causation, or a result.
- Visual resemblance to a shipped experience does not eliminate the margin of
  error or establish exact production equivalence.
- A recovery record does not prove that a visible Figma variant was approved or
  implemented.
- Flattened images, PDFs, and exports are historical artifacts, not active
  editable design.
- Mason chooses which material appears in the portfolio.
- Figma remains read-only unless Mason separately authorizes an edit.
