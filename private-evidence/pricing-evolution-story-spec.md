## Rejected candidate narrative

> As DeepL's pricing expanded from one Translator plan grid into four product
> and packaging models, Mason turned competitor research and messaging analysis
> into naming and content rules that gave cumulative tiers, parallel offers,
> prices, and limits a clear place.

The pivotal judgment is to define the commercial relationship before writing
the card, then give shared information one place and changing information a
consistent pattern. Present that as Mason's rationale and contribution, not
sole product or interaction ownership.

The case uses four movements:

1. **One grid became several buying systems.** Establish the original
   Translator grid, API's developer route, and Write's combined, standalone,
   and account-upgrade routes.
2. **Define the relationship before writing the card.** Connect competitor
   research and messaging analysis to value propositions, naming, and pricing
   structure. Use the add-on states as a project consequence Mason's content
   supported.
3. **State shared information once.** Use the repeated combined cards,
   cumulative Translator headings, and table-label states to give shared and
   changing information different jobs.
4. **Let each commercial model use the right pattern.** Resolve the title with
   Translator's cumulative ladder, Write's self-serve and business offers,
   Voice's parallel offers, and API's subscription, usage, and custom pricing.

The ending may say what the selected work demonstrates. It may not imply
shipment, adoption, testing results, causation, customer behaviour, or measured
outcomes.

## Evidence decision

No recovered artifact establishes a comparable same-offer chronology for the
repeated combined cards and the cumulative Translator cards. They are separate
descriptive states, never a Before and After transformation. The add-on states
are descriptive controls, not evidence that Mason designed the interaction.

The public story retains the original Translator grid, combined-route selector,
standalone Write and account-upgrade crop, add-on states, focused repeated-card
row, cumulative Translator row, table-label pair, and Write, Voice, and API
patterns. The redundant Translator grid and two narrow bundle details are
preserved under the repository archive route.
