1. Introduction
Our brand guidelines are designed to ensure a consistent and cohesive representation of our brand across all platforms and materials.
Adhering to these guidelines helps to build and maintain a strong brand identity.
Why is it important for DeepL to have a strong & consistent brand identity?
Especially as we move to a B2B model, aiming to partner with large, established enterprises, we need DeepL to be perceived as a serious,
professional player in the market. Our external image and reputation matters now more than ever. We want to present ourselves to the
world in a certain way, so that our clients and prospects take us seriously and want to do business with us.
If you use the wrong colours, font or logo in a presentation to external stakeholders for example, it may not seem like a big deal, but multiply
this by the number of people at DeepL and the number of presentations being created, there is a real risk that our image becomes diluted,
and we convey the impression that our work is messy, unprofessional and unreliable.
That’s why we all have a role to play in keeping our brand identity consistent and our reputation intact.
DeepL is at the forefront of Language AI and digital language platforms.
Our brand identity puts linguistic expertise, transformative technology and the diversity of our customers at its heart.
The DeepL that our customers are familiar with has been revitalised to communicate the progressive, dynamic nature of DeepL’s products,
and provide the necessary tools for the brand to interact with our customers. DeepL’s brand identity creates a unified voice across all
brand applications. Whether social marketing or an investors keynote, it’s important that we look and speak like DeepL.
This page outlines guidance to use and express DeepL’s Brand in the best way.
Find the link to download assets at the end of this page!

2. DeepL logo
Central to the DeepL brand is the logo lockup. Its form is familiar to longstanding customers, and now tells the story of our platform’s ability
to dynamically change language, whether through translation or writing. Beyond the logo, this story of language mastery is communicated
through the used of Language Blocks/cubes with the same dynamic quality as the symbol. Our language cubes are part of our identity
derivates from our logo shape - embodies machine learning with a 3D cube viewed in the form of a hexagon. Each face of the cube shows a
different language, acting as a lens through which to see language in a different way.
Our official logo lockup
Our logo represents our brand and should always remain true-to-form. It tells a story about who we are and what we do. The hexagon
shape is technical, suggesting data and modularity, with a speech bubble detail symbolizing our commitment to innovation and effective
communication.

Logo safe area
When using the logo in a design or placing it next to other visual elements, you should ensure that it has plenty of room to breathe. This is
where clear space and margins come in to play.

Please do not place the logo’s margins any tighter — in certain instances, the space can be increased.
Please make sure no visual elements or page boundaries overlap the safe area.
This safe area must be applied to any variation of the logo.

Logo anatomy
Our logo lockup consists of two elements: our symbol and our word mark. Our standard practice is to use the complete logo lockup as
the primary brand representation. However there are instances where we are allowed to use our symbol separately.
Logo official colours
The logo lockup can be used in only 3 colors, depending on context. Our Deep Blue is the primary color, the secondary one is White.
Sapphire Blue-400 is used for dark mode reserved for digital applications and exceptional cases for printing.
Treatment
The Primary style is the preferred choice for most applications and backgrounds. Diapositive when the Primary version lacks legibility,
especially against image backgrounds that are dark or complex. The Dark mode version is available for use on Deep Blue colored

3. Color Palette
DeepL's color palette emphasizes the prominence of blue, alongside a modern pairing with a vibrant purple. Beyond this, the wider palette
aims to create accessible combinations, ensuring a contemporary, visually cohesive design language in all applications. The Neutral palette
has light mode tones (Quarts gray) for use on light backgrounds, and Dark mode tones (Slate Gray) for use on dark backgrounds.

4. Typography
Primary typeface: Aktiv Grotesk
Aktiv Grotesk is our primary typeface, offering versatility, functionality, and support for ten global writing systems, making it the perfect
choice for our brand identity. We use variable font for most writing systems except from JP, KR, and ZH we use opentype font weights.

Aktiv Grotesk is a paid font exclusively licensed by the type foundry Dalton Maag. We utilize this font exclusively for marketing and
branding purposes.

Secondary Typeface: Roboto
On web and within digital product, we elevate Roboto for use as the headline typeface. Matching weighting and spacing with Aktiv Grotesk.
Use Roboto in Google Suite Apps as it is a Google native font (G-docs, G-slides, G-sheets, etc.)
Roboto should not be used in any marketing, physical or printed application that doesn’t require use of a webfont.
Additional font: Noto Sans
The following languages are not supported by Roboto, please use instead:
Japanese: Noto Sans JP
Korean: Noto Sans KR
Chinese: Noto Sans SC
Noto is a native typeface in most of Google Suite Apps.
5. Key Visuals: Language Blocks
Our language blocks are a core representation of linguistic interaction and DeepL’s digital product. The cubes can feature hero copy
addressing specific topics, convey broader DeepL themes such as universal greetings, or be a general representation of language AI.

Flat language blocks offer a versatile range of applications, from highly functional to expressive uses. Their adaptability allows for a broad
spectrum of usage while maintaining consistency in the overall identity.
6. Iconography
Our iconography is clear and functional, with a subtle underline stylisation that creates a connection to the brand illustration style. The
underline represents the DeepL platform, as seen in isometric form as the basis for illustrations. These icons are designed to be used only in
Marketing and Communication. Product Icons use a different library.
Don’t use icons from unapproved sources; only utilize marketing icons that have been reviewed and approved by the designers from
Creative Studio.

7. Illustration Style
We use an isometric grid as the fundament for our illustrations. Our illustrations primarily function as pictographic visualizations, supporting
accompanying copy. The level of scale and complexity is determined based on the story and context where it’s applied. When multiple
illustrations are used together, consistency is key—ensuring singular line weights for a cohesive visual experience. We use an isometric grid
as the fundament for our illustrations.
Do not resize the original icon sizes or use them as illustrations. Refrain from using icons to convey complex information or using
them without proper context.
If you need to modify icons, please request a review from the Creative Studio team.

Make sure that our pairings always have the right amount of contrast. Make sure that the weight of color feels well distributed. Here you find
some examples that work well.
8. Photography Style
Capture real-world experiences to create authentic and relatable brand narratives, enhancing the human touch of our brand. Showcasing
genuine moments that connect our audience to our product. Maintain cohesive art direction across photography categories. Through
deliberate curation and warm tonalities, we incorporate color and vibrancy to our brand. When showcasing our customers, the aim is to
capture imagery that feels spontaneous and in the moment.
Avoid staged appearances and discourage direct eye contact with the camera. When appropriate, strive to establish a genuine connection
with our product.
