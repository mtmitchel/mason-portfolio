Executive Summary
1. Portfolio
● Most companies follow a structured tier system: a free tier, 2 paid self-serve tiers,
and an Enterprise plan for large organizations, especially in SaaS.
● 
Free plans often include basic features to attract individual users or small teams,
while paid tiers scale with additional features for larger teams or businesses.
● 
Enterprise plans typically offer customization, scalability, and additional
administrative tools, as well as analytics or infrastructure capabilities.
2. Packaging
● Common packaging strategies include "Good/Better/Best" models, with features
increasing in functionality and complexity across tiers, allowing customers to start
on simpler/free plans and go up as their needs change or become more
sophisticated.
● 
Paid tiers often differ by the number of users, admin features, admin controls,
security and additional usage limits (e.g., data or translation capacities).
● 
Add-ons are commonly available, at times limited only to sales-assisted channels.
3. GTM Insights
● Ability to self-serve purchase the plans is the standard for free and lower-tier options,
while Enterprise plans usually require sales engagement for customization.
● 
Online pricing is typical for free and mid-tier plans, while Enterprise plans tend to use
contact-sales models with custom pricing.
● 
Most companies either list a minimum or maximum number of seats per plan.

● 
There seems to be overlap between channels, meaning some plans can be available
for both self-service and sales-assisted.
4. Messaging
● Plan names are pretty standard across companies with their “Free” option often
being called to simply as “Free or Basic”, their entry plan being something like
“Starter or Pro” and then their second tier often being “Business or Organization” and
the Standard across all companies has been “Enterprise” for their Enterprise offering.
● 
Descriptions frequently include "Who is this for?" messaging to guide customers
based on their needs (e.g., individuals, small teams, enterprises).
5. Pricing
● SaaS plans are typically priced per user per month but often the annual price per seat
is also stated online.
● 
Annual billing is often incentivized with discounts with almost all companies offering
significant discounts for annual billing (ranging from 8% at the low end to 40% at the
very highest end). Typical annual discount is closer to 20%.
● 
Most pricing models are transparent online, especially for non-Enterprise tiers, and
are shown in multiple currencies (with a minimum of USD and EUR)
● 
Entry plans (Starter/Pro) range between 7-20EUR with the average being ~14EUR.
● 
Second tier plans (Business/Organization) range between 20-45 with the average
being ~30EUR. This makes the jump between tiers an approx 100% price increase.
● 
Free trials (typically 14–30 days) are common.
