Guide for UX Writers Crafting Upgrade/Upsell Modals
This guide is for UX copywriters creating upgrade/upsell modals to encourage free DeepL users
to subscribe to DeepL Pro.
Understanding the Purpose
Upgrade/upsell modals are a type of microcopy that aim to persuade users to upgrade to a paid
plan to access more features or benefits. They should be written to be compelling, concise, and
benefit-driven.
Understanding DeepL's Context
DeepL's Goal: Increase the number of users who subscribe to DeepL Pro. Reason: The rate of
growth of Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) from self-service subscriptions has been slowing.
Target Audience: Existing DeepL free users actively using the platform but yet to upgrade.
Desired Outcome: Motivate more users to purchase DeepL Pro and increase the session →
Translate trial conversion rate. Challenges: Existing competitor improvements, like Google
Translate's quality enhancements, require a stronger demonstration of DeepL's value
proposition. Additionally, DeepL needs to create a user journey optimized for diverse user
segments and markets as the platform expands into 165 new markets.
Knowing Your Audience
Identify the specific user segment the modal is targeting. For DeepL, the target audience is
existing free users actively using the platform who have yet to upgrade. This audience can be
further segmented by their needs and usage patterns. Understand the needs, wants, and
pain points of this target audience. What are their goals? What challenges do they face?
DeepL's target audience includes:
● Decision-makers: Concerned with return on investment (ROI). Messaging should
highlight cost savings, efficiency improvements, and quantifiable benefits.
● End users: Includes both new and existing users of DeepL's products.
○ New users: Need to understand how DeepL enables "better expression" and the
benefits of the various products.
○ Existing users: Need continued engagement and encouragement to explore
new features and integrations.
● Potential customers: Professionals seeking translation services or writing support.
Messaging should focus on addressing pain points, offering solutions, and highlighting
DeepL's unique value proposition. Use this information to tailor your copy to their
specific needs and motivations.
Overcoming DeepL's Challenges

● User Blindness: Some users are not aware of DeepL's features and capabilities,
particularly those outside of the Translator UI.
● User Inertia: Some users prioritize convenience and stick with familiar tools even if they
are not the best option.
● User Irritation: Excessive pop-ups and promotions can negatively impact the user
experience and drive users away.
Objectives for Upgrade/Upsell Modal Copy
● Showcase Value: Demonstrate DeepL Pro's added value compared to the free version
and competitors.
● Drive Activation: Encourage users to engage with DeepL Translate and its features.
● Boost Conversions: Optimize the 'try-then-buy' user journey to convince free users to
subscribe to DeepL Pro.
● Enhance User Experience: Ensure a smooth and enjoyable user journey by avoiding
disruptive pop-ups and providing helpful, contextual guidance.
● Address User Needs: Tailor messaging to different user segments and their specific
needs and pain points.
● Competitive Differentiation: Highlight DeepL's advantages, particularly in quality,
curated training data, and post-editing features.
Best Practices and Guidelines for Upgrade/Upsell Modal Copy
The Purpose of Modals
● Strategically interrupt users at key moments to present a relevant offer.
● Clearly communicate DeepL Pro's benefits, differentiating it from free and
competitor offerings.
● Encourage immediate upgrade action.
Placement and Timing
Trigger modals contextually, based on user actions such as:
● Reaching or exceeding usage limits (e.g., document translation, character limit).
● Attempting to use a Pro-only feature (e.g., formality adjustment).
● Returning multiple times daily or copying numerous translations.
● Show relevant promotions based on translated file format or repetitive actions.
Other considerations for placement and timing:
● Avoid disrupting the core task.
● Minimize annoyance by carefully considering frequency and timing.
● Remove the "Nag Modal" that triggers after 40 translations within 24 hours, as it is
ineffective and contradicts DeepL's premium branding.

● Introduce users to integrations contextually, avoiding disruptive pop-ups.
● Timing is crucial. Trigger the modal at a point in the user journey when they are most
likely to be receptive to an upgrade offer. This could be when they have hit a usage limit
or have just experienced the value of a premium feature.
● Context is key. Ensure that the modal's content is relevant to the user's current activity
or the features they have been using.
Messaging and Tone
Overall tone should be:
● Direct and Actionable: Use concise, encouraging language that promotes immediate
action.
● Benefit-Oriented: Emphasize the value users gain from upgrading.
● Addressing Specific Needs: Tailor messaging to individual user context and usage
patterns.
● Highlight Key Differences: Articulate what sets DeepL Pro apart from the free version
and competitors.
DeepL's brand voice and tone should be:
● Helpful: Provide solutions to user pain points, anticipating their questions.
● Empathetic: Acknowledge and understand user frustrations with limitations and
concerns.
● Self-assured: Confidently position DeepL Pro as the superior solution.
● Professional: Maintain a courteous and respectful tone.
● Conversational: Use natural, everyday language, addressing the user directly.
Messaging should focus on:
● Increased Usage Limits: DeepL Pro allows more documents per month and more
characters per document than the free version.
● Advanced Features: Access to formality adjustment, glossaries, and other Pro-only
features.
● Seamless Integrations: Streamlined workflow with integrations into popular tools like
Microsoft Word.
● Enhanced Security: Data protection and other security benefits exclusive to paid
accounts.
When writing copy, remember to:
● Highlight Benefits and Value Proposition: Emphasize what the user will gain by
upgrading, rather than just listing the features of the higher-tier plan. Frame the benefits
in terms of how they will help the user achieve their goals or overcome their challenges.
Use numbers and data to quantify the benefits.

● Create a Sense of Urgency: Use phrases like "limited-time offer" or highlight that the
user is missing out on exclusive benefits.
● Include Social Proof: Use customer testimonials or reviews to increase the
persuasiveness of your copy.
Microcopy Strategies
Headline:
● Concisely state the primary benefit of upgrading, addressing a specific pain point.
● Use bolding sparingly to emphasize essential words or phrases.
● See examples in source.
Body Copy:
● Expand on the headline, elaborating on the value proposition while being succinct.
● Use powerful verbs and action-oriented language.
● Highlight 2-3 key benefits most relevant to the user's context.
● Preemptively address potential objections and concerns.
● Consider using bullet points for easier readability.
● See examples in source.
Call to Action:
● Use strong verbs that encourage immediate action.
● Focus on the value users receive.
● Clearly state the desired outcome.
● See examples in source.
Visuals:
● Use engaging visuals that reinforce the message.
● Consider before-and-after comparisons showcasing Pro feature benefits.
● Utilize icons to represent key benefits.
Terminology:
● Use "free version" and "paid version" to communicate feature limitations and data
protection benefits of paid accounts.
● Use "plan" when referring to the different subscription options available.
● Use "subscription" once a plan has been purchased.
● "Upgrade" refers to changes within DeepL Pro, such as increasing the number of users
or changing to a higher subscription plan.
● "Subscribe" encourages users to sign up for DeepL Pro for the first time.
● Refer to DeepL Pro's paid plans as "Starter", "Advanced", and "Ultimate".

● Avoid using "Pro" on its own when referring to a specific plan, as this can be ambiguous.
Instead, use "DeepL Pro Starter," "DeepL Pro Advanced," or "DeepL Pro Ultimate."
● Be Consistent: Ensure consistency in terminology and messaging throughout the
product and website.
Additional Guidelines
● Keep the User in Mind: Understand the user's perspective, their needs, and their
potential pain points.
● Be Succinct: Provide only the information necessary for the user to understand and
take action.
● Use Active Voice: Enhance readability and clarity by using active voice whenever
possible.
● Prioritize Accessibility: Ensure the microcopy is accessible to users with disabilities,
including those using screen readers.
● Make it Easy to Dismiss: Users should be able to close the modal quickly if they are
not interested. However, consider adding a secondary call to action, such as "Learn
more" or "Remind me later."
● Don't be Pushy: Avoid aggressive language or tactics that could annoy users.

Testing and Iteration
● A/B test variations of headlines, body copy, and calls to action to optimize conversion.
● Gather user feedback on modal content and placement to ensure effectiveness and
minimize disruption.
● Continuously refine and enhance messaging based on data and insights.
● Use A/B testing to experiment with different headlines, body copy, calls to action,
and even the design of the modal.
● Track your conversion rates and make adjustments based on the data.
