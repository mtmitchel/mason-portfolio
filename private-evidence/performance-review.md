## Purpose

This master removes repeated wording, AI-expanded claims, duplicated metrics, and future-development material from the recovered performance-review files. It preserves the useful facts once, identifies the strongest source for each fact, and records what the files do and do not prove.

The source order used here is:

1. contemporaneous project records and Mason-authored corrections in the DeepL achievement recovery;
2. original HiBob self-reflection exports under `source-documents/`;
3. derivative Markdown reflection drafts;
4. general DeepL performance and goal-setting guidance.

A self-reflection proves that Mason recorded a contribution or result in the performance process. It does not independently prove analytics, causation, manager approval, submission status, launch, or sole ownership.

## Deduplicated achievement evidence

### Enterprise landing-page program

- **What survives:** Mason contributed copy to more than 25 product, enterprise, and developer landing pages, including a major Enterprise-page revamp. He worked with designers, product managers, PMM, SEO, and implementation partners.
- **Strongest new pages:** `source-documents/performance-review/1st half bob.pdf`, pages 1-2; `source-documents/performance-review/Bob.pdf`, pages 1-2.
- **Supported scope:** More than 25 pages and an on-schedule program are repeated in the self-reflections and supported by the existing project record.
- **Not established:** Program leadership, sole ownership, authorship of reusable blocks, or personal causation of engagement and sales-inquiry changes.
- **Recovery relationship:** Strengthens `CEX-01` and corroborates `CEX-03`; it does not resolve `CEX-02` completion gaps.

### Desktop and integration content

- **What survives:** Mason supported desktop-app promotion and product positioning, and the broader recovery documents his copy work across desktop and integration landing pages.
- **Strongest new pages:** `source-documents/performance-review/1st half bob.pdf`, page 2; `source-documents/performance-review/Bob.pdf`, page 2.
- **Not established:** That Mason's copy caused 4.19 million Windows downloads, 1.95 million macOS downloads, 25% active-user growth, or 10% feature-engagement growth. These appear as platform-level figures in self-review prose without attribution reporting.
- **Recovery relationship:** Corroborates `APP-03`; it does not replace the stronger qualitative experiment evidence for `APP-01`.

### Pro Driver messaging

- **What survives:** Mason collaborated with Product Design, Content Design, and Monetization on benefit-led Pro Driver messaging for free-to-paid conversion work.
- **Strongest new pages:** `source-documents/performance-review/1st half bob.pdf`, page 2; `source-documents/performance-review/Bob.pdf`, pages 2-3.
- **Conflict:** The self-reflections use 12% conversion growth and €1.2M ARR, while the existing recovery preserves a separate missing-canvas formulation of 124 additional purchases per day and approximately €3.2M projected ARR.
- **Current resolution:** Keep the contribution and use Mason's approved high-level formulation—12% lift in paid conversion and a seven-figure ARR uplift—only for the overall Pro Driver/free-to-paid experiment wave. Retire the conflicting granular formulations rather than combining them.
- **Recovery relationship:** Corroborates the contribution in `MON-01` but does not raise its evidence level.

### SEO-informed landing-page optimization

- **What survives:** Mason translated changing SEO briefs, keyword priorities, and content requirements into user-centered landing-page copy while preserving product accuracy and conversion intent.
- **Strongest new pages:** `source-documents/performance-review/1st half bob.pdf`, page 2; `source-documents/performance-review/Bob.pdf`, pages 2-3.
- **Current scope:** The self-reflections do not independently prove the claimed 15% organic-traffic increase, 30% conversion increase, and €800,000 revenue contribution. Mason confirmed that the supplied figures came from DeepL BI; they may be used as program context, not as the result of one page or Mason's sole causation.
- **Recovery relationship:** Corroborates `SEO-01` without resolving its missing-analytics status.
