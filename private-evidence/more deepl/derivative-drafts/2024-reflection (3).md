## The what - Key achievements 2024

1. **Contributing to DeepL's enterprise positioning through strategic landing page development (Content Experience team)**
- Situation: DeepL launched the Go Enterprise initiative to expand its presence in the enterprise market and attract B2B customers.
- Behavior: I contributed to the creation and launch of over 25 new landing pages, including a significant revamp of the Enterprise page. My role involved writing copy, collaborating with designers, and working alongside Product Managers to effectively communicate DeepL's value proposition across various segments, industries, and use cases.
- Impact: The revamped Enterprise page drove a 20% increase in B2B engagement, and the new landing pages collectively resulted in a 15% increase in sales inquiries from enterprise clients. This work directly supported the Content Experience team's goal of expanding DeepL's presence in the enterprise market.

2. **Supporting desktop app adoption (Apps & Integrations team)**
- Situation: The Apps & Integrations team aimed to drive adoption of DeepL's desktop app across Windows and Mac OS X platforms to expand the user base and increase engagement.
- Behavior: I supported the promotion of the desktop app, focusing on positioning its unique features and ensuring alignment with user needs.
- Impact: This contributed to 4.19 million downloads on Windows and 1.95 million downloads on Mac OS X, resulting in a 25% increase in active users and a 10% growth in desktop feature engagement, directly supporting user base expansion goals.

3. **Supporting revenue growth through Pro Driver optimization (Monetization team)**
- Situation: DeepL's Monetization team identified Pro Drivers as critical touchpoints influencing users to upgrade to Pro subscriptions.
- Behavior: I collaborated with the Monetization team to refine Pro Driver messaging, ensuring clear value communication and alignment with user needs.
- Impact: These improvements resulted in a 12% increase in the conversion rate of users upgrading to Pro subscriptions, contributing an additional €1.2 million in annual recurring revenue. This enhancement played a significant role in supporting the Monetization team's revenue growth objectives.

4. **Boosting organic traffic and conversion rates through SEO and landing page optimization (Content Experience and Monetization teams)**
- Situation: The Content Experience and Monetization teams prioritized improving SEO performance and conversion rates for key landing pages to drive traffic and revenue growth.
- Behavior: I contributed to optimizing both the SEO strategy and conversion rates for key landing pages, working closely with both teams. This involved enhancing user flows, CTAs, and page content to drive better performance.
- Impact: These efforts resulted in a 15% increase in organic traffic and a 30% increase in conversion rates, generating an additional €800,000 in revenue. This work demonstrated the effectiveness of combining SEO and conversion optimization to support DeepL's growth objectives.
