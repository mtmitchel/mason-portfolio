## Account-security and administration decisions

- The account-security candidate is now one complete **MFA account recovery**
  journey rather than a mixed collection of personal and team settings. The
  public sequence runs from the normal login and failed-code states through
  recovery-code verification, reset guidance and an explicit active state.
- The credential-bearing MFA setup frame is intentionally excluded from public
  assets because it exposes a manual authenticator secret. The case explains
  the required re-enrollment step without publishing that credential.
- The administration candidate is now one complete **bulk user deletion**
  journey rather than a sampler of unrelated bulk actions. The six selected
  frames show the existing user-management context, inactive-user filter,
  selected set, bulk-action menu, access-loss confirmation and resulting state.
- The administration exports contain fictional `@domain.com` demo accounts,
  not employee or customer data.
- Both cases are artifact-led: they demonstrate interaction logic, content
  decisions and state transitions. Do not add claims about launch, adoption,
  reduced errors, support reduction or measured security outcomes.
- These remain two finished options for the single eventual account-security or
  administration portfolio slot; the private review route is the comparison
  surface for choosing between them.

## Writing and information hierarchy

- Use literal, recruiter-scannable project names. Do not use advertising hooks,
  metaphors, faux drama, or constructions such as “X happened; Y did not have
  to.”
- Case-study headlines should state the work and scope directly.
- Keep the homepage introduction to one concise role line. Do not repeat the
  same positioning in the section heading.

  a second message beside it.

  18px.
