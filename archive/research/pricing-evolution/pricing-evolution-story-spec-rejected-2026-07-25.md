## Corrected story summary

The case is a seven-chapter evolution story. It begins with Translator as the only product in the individual-and-team plan grid while API remains on a developer tab. Write then appears through three buying routes and three descriptions.

The middle chapters cover repeated self-serve bundle content, add-on states, cumulative headings already visible in the add-on-on state, and a row-label rewrite. The final chapter distinguishes Write’s cumulative offers, Voice’s parallel offers, and API’s cumulative subscription-and-usage pattern.

The closing describes selected visible patterns only. It makes no claim of shipment, later adoption, current architecture, user behavior, conversion, or revenue.

## Structural rules

1. The Translator plan-grid condition appears before the Write complication.
2. Chapter 2 explicitly names all three Write buying routes and all three visible product descriptions.
3. Bundle-wide claims name Starter, Advanced, and Ultimate or use `the three self-serve tiers`.
4. Enterprise is identified as a separate sales offer wherever a visible exception matters.
5. Chapter 4 covers the add-on control, combined prices, discount placement, and Enterprise behavior. It does not introduce cumulative headings.
6. Chapter 5 states that cumulative headings are already visible in the add-on-on state, then isolates the full-list-to-progression rewrite.
7. Chapter 6 distinguishes repeated nouns from repeated qualifiers, states that numerals are bold, and preserves intrinsic units.
8. Chapter 7 states that Write and API use cumulative headings and that Voice uses parallel offers.
9. Every material claim sits beside the image or image pair that proves it.
10. Alternative text describes the image’s purpose without duplicating the caption.
11. No sentence opens with a pronoun or deictic phrase whose antecedent exists only in an earlier paragraph.
12. No standalone caption or alternative text uses `the same` to refer to another state.
13. Public copy uses exact visible numerals, punctuation, colons, prices, and units when transcribing UI text.
14. First-person contribution is limited to pricing writing, naming rules, and content structure.
15. No public sentence claims shipment, adoption, current architecture, causation, metrics, sole ownership, relative maintenance cost, decision duration, or an internal source that settled a decision.
16. No public sentence exceeds 60 words.

## Claim ceilings applied

- Allowed: observable differences between the Translator plan grid and API tab, Write’s buying routes and names, repeated self-serve bundle content, add-on states, cumulative headings, comparison-table row labels, and product-specific card logic.
- Allowed: Mason’s contribution to pricing writing, naming rules, and content structure.
- Allowed: visible prices, discounts, and usage rates as offer content.
- Not allowed: claims that a selected version shipped, became current, was later adopted, improved conversion, caused revenue, or changed universal user behavior.
- Not allowed: sole ownership of product strategy or interaction design.
- Not allowed: relative maintenance cost, elapsed decision time, or claims that one source settled an internal decision.
