## Scoring and order

First apply a viability floor. A candidate needs hiring-reader value, a
defensible account of Mason's contribution, and enough linked evidence for one
bounded story check. A Figma frame or mockup cannot make an otherwise weak or
unsupported candidate viable. Candidates with story readiness `0` remain in
reserve regardless of their visual score.

Score each candidate from `0` to `2` on reader value, story readiness, and
Figma-backed site-visual readiness:

- `2` means an exact or closely corresponding native Figma frame, mockup, or
  preserved Figma export can serve a clear evidence, explanation, or comparison
  role on the site and at least one known export is recruiter-ready or needs
  only a bounded crop, background, or annotation removal. A cleaner existing
  repository export counts when its Figma relationship is known.
- `1` means the relationship is contextual or adjacent, or the known exports
  need a clean re-export or substantial reconstruction before they can support
  the central story.
- `0` means no corresponding Figma visual is identified, or every known export
  is blank, incomplete, duplicated from another candidate, or unable to do a
  clear job in this candidate's story. A flattened chat attachment without a
  recoverable Figma frame can supplement later evidence, but it does not receive
  Figma-backed visual credit.

Story readiness `2` means there are enough linked sources for one bounded story
check; it does not mean the story is already verified. Visual readiness measures
both story usefulness and practical portfolio presentation. It does not select
an asset or prove contribution, lifecycle, shipment, or results.

For tied totals among viable candidates, prefer Figma-backed site-visual
readiness, then reader value, then story readiness, then the prior queue order.
New CV candidates follow CV order; new Figma candidates follow
extraction-inventory order. Existing public coverage and Mason's frozen work
override every score.
