   We care

   • Situation: While working on the revamp of the Enterprise landing pages, I realized
   that our existing content didn’t fully address the specific challenges faced by potential
   B2B customers. I wanted to ensure that our messaging was not only informative but
   also empathetic to the unique needs of these users.

   • Action: I collaborated closely with Product Managers and designers to rewrite the
   content in a way that directly acknowledged and addressed the challenges faced by
   our target audience. This included adding dedicated sections that highlighted how
   DeepL’s solutions could alleviate their pain points, whether they were a small business
   or a large enterprise. I also incorporated feedback from free users to ensure that their
   needs were considered, showing our commitment to all users.

   • Outcome: The revised content led to a 15% increase in engagement on the Enterprise
   page, as measured by time spent on the page and a reduction in bounce rates.
   Additionally, there was a 10% increase in inquiries from potential enterprise clients.
   This outcome not only improved user satisfaction but also demonstrated our
   dedication to understanding and meeting the needs of all our users, aligning with
   DeepL’s value of caring for users and customers.

   We build for scale, quality, and think long-term

   • Situation: Our team was facing challenges with consistently producing high-quality
   content for multiple landing pages and other touchpoints. The existing processes were
   not scalable enough to handle the growing demands, which was affecting our ability
   to maintain DeepL’s high standards.

   • Action: To address this, I proposed and helped implement a new content creation
   workflow that was designed to be scalable and efficient. This workflow included
   standardized templates, clear roles and responsibilities, and a more streamlined
   review process. The goal was to reduce bottlenecks, ensure consistency, and allow the
   team to scale content production while maintaining the quality DeepL is known for.
• Outcome: The adoption of this workflow improved the efficiency of our content
creation process and significantly enhanced the consistency and quality of our
outputs. This new process enabled us to launch over 25 new landing pages on
schedule, supporting DeepL’s long-term goal of scaling our content efforts while
maintaining our commitment to quality.

We are decisive, move fast, and act as owners

• Situation: The deadline for launching the revamped Enterprise landing page was
approaching rapidly, but there were delays in finalizing the copy due to evolving
brand guidelines. This was putting the entire project at risk of missing its deadline.

• Action: Recognizing the urgency, I took ownership of the situation by quickly
aligning with key stakeholders to finalize the content. I made necessary adjustments to
ensure the copy aligned with the new brand guidelines and pushed for expedited
reviews. I also coordinated closely with the design and development teams to ensure
that the content was implemented smoothly and without further delays.

• Outcome: The Enterprise page was successfully launched on time, contributing to a
successful product promotion that was aligned with our overall enterprise strategy.
This timely launch played a crucial role in achieving a 20% increase in B2B
engagement shortly after the page went live. My ability to act decisively and take
ownership of the situation ensured that we met our objectives without compromising
on quality.

We are humble, open, and collaborative

• Situation: While working on the landing page optimization project, I was part of a
cross-functional team that included members from content, design, and development.
Given the complexity of the project, it was important to ensure that all team members
were aligned and that communication was clear and open.

• Action: I actively fostered open communication within the team by regularly sharing
updates, asking for feedback, and being receptive to suggestions from all team
members, regardless of their role or seniority. I created a shared document to track
progress and challenges, ensuring that everyone had access to the information they
needed. I also made it a point to respect and consider ideas from all team members,
recognizing that the best solutions often come from collaborative efforts.

• Outcome: This collaborative approach led to a smoother workflow, with fewer
bottlenecks and a more cohesive final product. The landing pages we developed as a
team saw a 30% increase in conversions, demonstrating the effectiveness of our
collective efforts. My actions reflected DeepL’s value of humility and collaboration, as
the success of the project was truly a team effort.
